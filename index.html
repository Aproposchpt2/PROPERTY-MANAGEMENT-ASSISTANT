05 / QUESTIONS WORTH ANSWERING

Understand the operating model before you implement it.

Does PMA contact emergency services automatically?+

No. PMA is not a substitute for 911 or emergency services. The intended service follows approved safety instructions and property-specific notification and escalation workflows.

Are notifications guaranteed to reach everyone?+

No delivery should be represented as confirmed without independent carrier and recipient evidence. Notification submission, delivery and acknowledgment are distinct outcomes.

Can PMA help with leasing inquiries?+

PMA can present verified property information and collect showing requests. A tour is only confirmed when the approved scheduling system verifies it.

Can it create maintenance work orders?+

The assistant can collect the issue, location and urgency. A completed work order is confirmed only after an authoritative property system reports success.

Does property management remain in control?+

Yes. Property teams define approved information, escalation policies, contacts, operating boundaries and access to integrated systems.
