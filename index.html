01 / WHY NOW

Businesses are adding intelligence to their workforce.

Across service industries, routine first contact (answering, gathering details, routing, confirming) is moving to intelligent business systems. People are being realigned toward the work that needs judgment. Property management is built on exactly this kind of repeatable, around-the-clock contact.

01

Demand doesn't keep office hours.

Leaks, lockouts and heating failures happen at night and on weekends. Residents expect an answer when they call, not a voicemail box and a callback the next business day.

02

Staff time goes to repetition.

Much of a property team's day is spent taking down the same details, answering the same questions and relaying messages. That is predictable work, and predictable work can be systematized.

03

Intelligence fills the gap, people keep control.

An intelligent system handles intake, routing and status 24/7. Your team sets the rules, makes the decisions, and handles the exceptions and relationships.

05 / QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Does property management stay in control?+

Yes. Your team defines approved information, escalation rules, contacts, vendor pre-authorizations and what the assistant may do.

Does PMA replace my staff?+

No. It takes over repetitive intake and relay work so your people can focus on decisions, residents and vendors.

Does PMA call 911?+

No. PMA is not a substitute for emergency services. It follows your safety instructions and notification workflows.
