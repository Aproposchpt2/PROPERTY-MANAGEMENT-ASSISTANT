TECHNOLOGY HANDLES THE PREDICTABLE. PROPERTY TEAMS HANDLE THE EXCEPTIONAL.
WHY BUSINESSES ARE CHANGING

The workforce now includes intelligence.

Companies in every service industry are moving routine first contact (answering, recording details, routing, confirming) to intelligent business systems, and realigning their people toward work that needs judgment. Property management, with its around-the-clock resident calls, is one of the clearest fits.

Intelligence handles the predictable. Your team handles the exceptional.

WHO BENEFITS

A convenience for every party.

One system answers the calls, keeps the record and shares the status, so owners, managers, maintenance teams and residents all work from the same information.

Owners: lower costs, protected assets+

Every resident call is answered without paying for an after-hours answering service, on-call staff or overtime to cover the phones. Urgent problems such as leaks and heating failures reach your team the moment they are reported, while damage is still small and repairs cost less.

Vendor spending stays under your control. Pre-authorization rules let routine work proceed within limits you set, and anything above a not-to-exceed amount waits for a manager's approval. Faster, more consistent service also helps keep residents renewing, which protects occupancy and revenue.

Property managers: a dashboard instead of a ringing phone+

Your staff no longer spend the day answering calls, taking down the same details and relaying messages. Every request arrives already recorded, with the property, unit, issue, urgency and a case number.

The Management Dashboard shows open cases across your properties in one place. Managers update status, review residents' contact preferences and approve vendor work from a phone or computer. The time once spent on the phone goes to leasing, inspections, vendor oversight and the residents who need personal attention.

Residents: updates without calling back+

Residents reach a real answer any hour of the day or night, get a case number before the call ends and choose to be updated by text or email.

The Tenant Portal gives them a secure link to see the status of their request at any time. They no longer have to keep calling the office to make sure their issue is being worked on, and your office no longer has to take those calls.

Maintenance teams and vendors: the full picture first+

Every request arrives with the location, the problem, its urgency and how to access the unit, so technicians show up prepared. Fewer return trips and fewer clarifying phone calls mean more work completed each day.

THE SOLUTION

Property Management Assistant, from call to resolution.

STEP 01 OF 04

Resident calls

The assistant answers your property line any hour of the day or night, every day of the year. Residents speak naturally and are never sent to voicemail.

STEP 02 OF 04

Case opened

The assistant records the property, unit, issue, urgency and access details, then gives the resident a case number before the call ends.

STEP 03 OF 04

Team notified

Your designated manager and maintenance contacts are alerted right away, and approved vendors can be engaged within the pre-authorization limits you set.

STEP 04 OF 04

Status tracked

Managers update the case in the Management Dashboard. Residents follow progress through the Tenant Portal and text or email updates, without calling the office.
