PROPERTY MANAGEMENT ASSISTANT · FEATURES

Everything PMA does, on one page.

Features are configured per property. Your team decides what the assistant answers, who is notified and what requires approval.

RESIDENT EXPERIENCE
01

24/7/365 voice assistant

A natural-sounding assistant answers your property line every hour of every day, including nights, weekends and holidays.

ALWAYS ON
02

Incident & maintenance intake

Captures address, unit, issue description and urgency, then issues a case number before the call ends.

STRUCTURED INTAKE
03

Case status by phone

Residents check an existing case by caller ID or case number, with clear answers when there are several cases or none.

SELF-SERVICE
04

SMS & email confirmations

Residents choose how to be updated and receive confirmation by text or email.

RESIDENT CHOICE

Online status link

A secure, read-only link lets residents see their request status without calling the office.

RESIDENT PORTAL
06

Leasing inquiries
